The Issue

The dominant approach to reducing screen time is built on prevention and a distrust of your future self.

Parents confiscate phones. Adults install app blockers. These methods cause you to become reliant on them to solve the problem.

Restriction can reduce access in the moment, but it does not always help people practice self-regulation for the moments when access returns.

PhonePact explores another path: strengthening awareness and agency with support from people you trust.

We are building PhonePact around that design thesis.
