Help

Support

A real person reads every message.

Write to support@getphonepact.com and we’ll get back to you. There is no ticket system and no bot — PhonePact is small, and the person who replies is the person who built the thing you’re writing about.

Report something in a circle

If someone in your circle writes something abusive, or their name or photo is offensive, you can act on it inside the app without waiting for us:

  • Report a note — press and hold it in the Room, then choose Report or block.
  • Report a person — open the Circle tab, tap their name, then choose Report. Use this when someone’s name or photo is the problem.
  • Block them — from either of those sheets. Their notes, their name, their photo and any quotes of them disappear immediately, and they can no longer send you a notification.

We review every report and act on objectionable content, which can mean removing it or suspending an account. If something needs a faster answer than the app can give, email us directly and say urgent in the subject line.

Manage your subscription

PhonePact is free for now during launch and beta. Pricing may change later; any paid plan and its terms will be shown before purchase. If a paid plan is introduced:

  • Cancel or change on iPhone: open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → PhonePact. On another platform, use that app store’s subscription page.
  • Restore a purchase on a new device: in the app, open the You tab and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by the store that billed you. For Apple purchases, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your account

In the app: You tab → Delete account. You’ll be asked to sign in again first. Your account and profile, circles, pact, ordinary room notes and check-in rows attributed to you, and reports you filed are deleted. Reply excerpts, reaction attributions, and block-list references tied to your account are scrubbed. Reports made about your account may be retained as safety records with an anonymous identifier, but copied text attributed to you is scrubbed. The deleted account itself is not recoverable.

If you can’t reach the app, email us from the address on the account and we’ll do it for you.

Screen usage isn’t measuring

On iPhone, PhonePact uses Apple’s Screen Time system. If permission is off, grant it in Settings:

  • Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → turn on PhonePact.
  • Then open PhonePact and tap Turn on Screen Time on the Home screen.

PhonePact needs iOS 17.4 or later. On earlier versions Apple counts usage from midnight regardless of when you set your pact, which would fire a day’s worth of check-ins at once.

For an Android development build, open Settings → Apps → Special app access → Usage access and allow PhonePact. Menu names vary by phone maker. Android is not publicly available yet.

What your circle can see

The crossed duration is the only usage information your circle receives — for example, “reached 3h today”. Never which apps, never a per-app breakdown, never your browsing. On iPhone, the private breakdown is computed inside Apple’s Screen Time sandbox. On Android, PhonePact computes it locally from Usage Access. In both cases, app and package identities stay on the phone and are not sent to our servers or your circle.

If the same account is signed into more than one phone, each phone keeps its own pact and recent history. Your circle sees a short random label such as “iPhone A1B2” beside that phone’s pact and check-ins so one phone’s Screen Time is never presented as another’s.

The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
